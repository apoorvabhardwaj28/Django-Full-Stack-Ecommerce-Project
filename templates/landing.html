FAQ

Common questions answered clearly

Find quick answers to the questions shoppers usually have before they place an order on MyStore.

Do I need to create an account before shopping?

Yes. Signing up or logging in gives you access to your wishlist, cart, checkout, order history, and other personalized shopping features.

Can I save products and buy them later?

Yes. You can add products to your wishlist and come back anytime to review your saved items before moving them to your cart.

Can I use coupons or discount offers at checkout?

Yes. Eligible coupons can be applied during checkout so customers can enjoy discounts and complete their purchase more confidently.

Will I be able to track my order after purchase?

Yes. After placing an order, you can check order updates, view invoice details, and manage available order actions from your account.
